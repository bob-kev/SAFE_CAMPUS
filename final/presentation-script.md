# SafeCampus - Presentation Script
## Slide 1 - Title
"Good morning. We're Group 9. We designed SafeCampus, a mobile app to help students and staff report emergencies fast."
## Slide 2 - Problem
"During an emergency, people don't know who to call or how to report. Existing methods are slow and give no feedback."
## Slide 3 - Objectives
"Report in 3 taps, auto-share location, confirm mistakes, show clear feedback, and protect privacy."
## Slide 4 - User Research
"80% didn't know the right contact. 100% said speed matters. 60% worried about privacy."
## Slide 5 - Personas
"Meet Maya (student needing speed/anonymity) and Daniel (staff needing location/status)."
## Slide 6 - Design Principles
"Learnability, Efficiency, Error Prevention, Feedback, Accessibility, Privacy, and Stress-aware design."
## Slide 7 - The Prototype
"12 screens: Home, Emergency Type, QuickTap, Report Details, Location, Call Security, Success, Report History, Status Detail, Safety Resources, Settings, Onboarding."
## Slide 8 - Key Features
"QuickTap (3 taps), Call Security (persistent button), Auto-Location, Anonymity Toggle, Report Tracking, Safety Resources."
## Slide 9 - Usability Testing
"5 participants completed 5 tasks. All completed without help."
## Slide 10 - Iterations
"Two iterations improved location editing, status visibility, confirmation wording, and added call button on success."
## Slide 11 - Conclusion
"SafeCampus meets all objectives. Future work: accessibility testing, real backend, integration with campus security."
## Slide 12 - Q&A
"Thank you! Any questions?"
