# SafeCampus - Final Report
**Course:** Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
**Group:** Group 9
**Institution:** Dedan Kimathi University of Technology
## 1. Introduction
SafeCampus is a mobile application prototype that lets university students and staff report emergencies quickly and clearly.
## 2. Problem Statement
During an emergency, people often don't know who to contact or what to say. Existing methods are slow and lack feedback.
## 3. Project Objectives
1. Report in 3 taps or fewer
2. Guide users through a clear, structured report
3. Auto-share location
4. Prevent false reports via confirmation
5. Give instant confirmation and status feedback
6. Ensure accessibility, privacy, and trust
## 4. User Research
Five users (3 students, 2 staff) were interviewed. Key findings: 80% didn't know the right contact; 100% valued speed; 60% worried about privacy.
## 5. Personas
**Maya** (student needing speed/anonymity) and **Daniel** (staff needing location/status updates).
## 6. Design Process
Low-fidelity sketches → 12 Balsamiq wireframes. HCI principles applied.
## 7. The Prototype
12 screens: Home, Emergency Type, QuickTap, Report Details, Location, Call Security, Success, Report History, Status Detail, Safety Resources, Settings, Onboarding.
## 8. Usability Testing
5 participants completed 5 tasks. All key tasks completed without help.
## 9. Findings & Changes
5 issues identified (mostly medium severity) and fixed in Iteration 2.
## 10. Conclusion
SafeCampus demonstrates user-centered design creating a fast, trustworthy emergency reporting tool.
