# SafeCampus - User Personas
## Persona 1: Maya (Primary - Student)
- **Age:** 20 | **Status:** 2nd-year university student
- **Context:** Lives off-campus, walks home late after library sessions.
- **Goals:** Feel safe; report incidents quickly and anonymously.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't know the right number to call; forms feel too long.
- **Needs:** Fast, one-thumb reporting; reassurance; option to stay anonymous.
- **Quote:** "If something happened at night, I just want to tap once and know someone got my report."
## Persona 2: Daniel (Secondary - Staff)
- **Age:** 45 | **Status:** University facilities staff
- **Context:** Works across campus; often first to notice hazards.
- **Goals:** Report maintenance/safety issues precisely with location.
- **Frustrations:** Too many steps; location often wrong; no feedback.
- **Needs:** Easy location tagging, clear categories, status tracking.
- **Quote:** "I need to tell them exactly where and what, without typing a long essay."
