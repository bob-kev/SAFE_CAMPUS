# SafeCampus - Research Instrument
## Participant Screening
- Age range
- Role (student / staff)
- Smartphone usage comfort
## Interview Questions
1. Have you ever needed to report an incident on campus?
2. What would you do first if you saw a fire or suspicious person?
3. What information do people usually need to give?
4. What would stop you from reporting?
5. What would make you more likely to report?
6. Would you prefer to remain anonymous?
7. If you could report with your phone in under a minute, would you use it?
## Questionnaire Items (1-5 scale)
- "I know exactly who to contact in an emergency."
- "Reporting incidents feels confusing."
- "I worry about what happens to my report."
- "A mobile app would make me more likely to report."
